## Monetization Strategy

### Revenue Streams
1. **Game Pass** (30% of revenue)
   - $4.99/month
   - 1.5x XP boost
   - Exclusive cosmetics

2. **Premium Chests** (40% of revenue)
   - Common: Free (ads)
   - Rare: 99¢
   - Legendary: $4.99

3. **Cosmetics** (20% of revenue)
   - Character skins: $4.99
   - Weapon skins: $2.99
   - Emotes: $0.99

4. **Battle Pass** (10% of revenue)
   - Free track
   - Premium: $9.99/season

This roadmap outlines a 2-year development plan to transform the game from a solid launch title into a thriving live-service game with millions of players. Success depends on:

1. **Quality** - Maintain high standards

3. **Content** - Regular updates and new features
4. **Marketing** - Consistent promotion
5. **Monetization** - Fair and balanced systems

With dedicated execution, Hack & Slash can become a top-grossing mobile game.
